Software

IC3PO — automatic verifier for distributed protocols; produced the first fully automatic proof of Lamport’s Paxos and enabled follow-on work including Sift (ATC’22) and the Bakery proof (FORTE’23).

AVR — word-level hardware model checker; 1st place at HWMCC 2020 (7 gold), medals at HWMCC 2024 & 2025; evaluated on 1000+ industrial designs; used in industry and academia.

TurboFuzzLLM — open-source LLM red-teaming fuzzer; ≥95% attack success on GPT-4o and GPT-4 Turbo with 3× fewer queries.

Contributor to Yices 2 (SMT solver) and Yosys (synthesis).
