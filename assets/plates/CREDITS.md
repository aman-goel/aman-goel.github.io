## Caption-honesty notes

- **`tiger-tally.jpg` is a type-match, not the named object.** The essay's prose discusses the Du
  tiger tally (杜虎符, Shaanxi History Museum). That object's only Commons image is CC BY-SA, so the
  plate shows a different tally of the same type. The caption names the pictured object, its date and
  its holder, which is what makes the claim checkable; it deliberately does **not** carry a disclaimer
  sentence (author's decision, 2026-07-25 — narrating our licensing constraints to the reader is noise).
  Accuracy is carried by naming what is pictured, not by apologising for it.

- **`aleppo-codex.jpg` shows folios that no longer exist.** The Aleppo Codex lost almost its entire
  Torah in 1947; the extant manuscript begins at Deuteronomy 28:17. This image is legitimate because it
  is a **pre-loss photograph** — Wickes reproduced these Genesis folios in 1887. The caption states the
  1887 date and the 1947 loss, both because it is true and because a knowledgeable reader who sees
  "Aleppo Codex, Genesis" without them will reasonably assume an error.
- **`hero-kultepe.jpg` is not the Susa envelope.** The hero originally depicted Louvre Sb 1932
  (Susa, ~3300 BC), whose photography is rights-managed via RMN and cannot be shown. The Kültepe
  case carries the same structural idea — a sealed container whose exterior authenticates its
  contents — roughly 1,400 years later. The kicker and caption say Kültepe, not Susa.
- **`rhind-plate15.jpg` is a facsimile plate**, not a photograph of the papyrus itself. It comes from
  Chace's published edition, which the essay already cites as reference 12.

- **`rigveda-newberry.jpg` is an early-19th-century copy of a much older text.** The India chapter is about
  *oral* transmission, which by definition leaves no artifact; this manuscript is the written witness
  downstream of that system, and the caption says so rather than implying the page is ancient. The Commons
  file is titled "MS2097" after the Sam Fogg dealer catalogue; the library shelfmark is ORMS 960.

## Not sourced

- **Rome / tessera nummularia (KHM Wien, Hilario, 26 BC).** Searched Wikimedia Commons for a

  ledger-versus-measurement diagram rather than a plate. Do not licence-purchase.

## Files

The six `*.jpg` in this directory are the **web derivatives** that ship: ~1600px on the long edge,
progressive JPEG, 1.3 MB for the whole set. Full-resolution originals are preserved in
`originals/` and are what to re-derive from if sizing changes.

Two edits were made to the derivatives, both recorded here so they are not mistaken for the source
images: `dugga-tablet.jpg` is cropped to remove a mount clip at the right edge and a modern pencil
annotation at the bottom; `hero-kultepe.jpg` is cropped to a landscape window that leaves margin for the
callout labels; `rhind-plate15.jpg` was converted from PNG. No other image has been
altered — no retouching, no colour grading, nothing removed from any object.
